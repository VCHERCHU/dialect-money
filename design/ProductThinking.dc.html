Clarity
A 72-year-old who speaks only Hokkien cannot tell whether the rider, fixed-deposit promo or investment pitch in front of her is worth her money.
Named person, named moment. Not “seniors lack financial literacy”.
Consequence
Not confusion — delegation. She misses what she is entitled to, and cannot evaluate what is offered to her.
Every money question waits for a child who visits, or for the person selling.
Cause
The guidance is not missing. It is text, in English, on websites built for someone who searches, reads and scrolls.
Five Whys below — the chain does not end at language.
Confirmation
Access to users is secondary research only. One two-minute Hokkien explainer, played to two or three seniors, two questions asked.
SingStat · IMDA Digital Society Report · MAS literacy surveys · CPF take-up.
Start with the whys
1
She cannot judge the offer — the only explanation she gets is the sales pitch.
2
Why only his? Because every trustworthy explanation exists as written English.
3
Why does that not reach her? She reads almost no English and does not browse or search.
4
Why is it published that way? A website is the delivery model, and it assumes a reader.
5
Why does no channel replace it? The one that reaches her runs through a person — and that person is nobody's product surface.
Outcomes over outputs
Output
Six explainers, three dialects, a video mode, an auto-publish pipeline.
Outcome
She has enough understanding to ask a real question — of the agent, the teller, or her own daughter — instead of nodding along.
Counter
100% of factual claims trace to a cited whitelisted page. The refusal screen is what that looks like in the interface — one confidently narrated wrong figure in Hokkien does more damage than the product does good.
Policy · Ops · Tech
Policy
Explains, never advises. Whitelisted sources only. Institutions named through the MAS Financial Institutions Directory and nowhere else.
Ops
A human accuracy check before anything publishes, and a named person — daughter, befriender, CC volunteer — who puts the link in front of her.
Tech
Retrieval decides what the model may see; it answers from those passages only and must say NO_ANSWER otherwise. Dialect speech in and out is still a stand-in — that, not the pipeline, is the open risk.
