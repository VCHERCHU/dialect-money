The wordless vocabulary
One picture per explainer so a title never has to be read, one glyph per control, and three sound states so she can see whether it is talking. Everything on a 24 or 32 grid, one stroke weight, and recolourable — which is why none of it is an emoji.

What could not be made wordless, and why. The spoken lines and the three questions are the product — removing those words would remove the thing she came for; they stay, read aloud, with the pictogram as the anchor. The dialect name (福建话) stays too: it is the one label she reliably reads, and no picture distinguishes Hokkien from Teochew. Everything else — 上一句, 停, 下一句, 慢一点, 按一下就开始, 停了, 听完了, 再听一次, 分享, 其他题目 — is now a shape.
