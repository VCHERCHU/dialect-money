Brass carries the mark, and only the mark. Jade is for links and states, never the mark. Every text pair here clears 4.5:1 — on a product built for eyes that are 72 years old, that floor is not negotiable. Government blue was the obvious choice and is deliberately skipped: this has to feel like a person talking, not another portal.
