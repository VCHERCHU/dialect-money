有人卖东西给你，不懂就问。用你的话问，用你的话答。

Prototype — the dialect layer is a stand-in. No browser recognises Hokkien or Teochew speech, and none speaks it. Listening uses your browser's Mandarin or Cantonese recognition, and answers use its Mandarin or Cantonese voice. Real dialect in and out is the MERaLiON strand of this project, and this page is the thing waiting on it — the interaction is real, the language is not yet. Answers are written by kimi-k3 through a local proxy that holds the API key — a key in this page would be readable by anyone. With no proxy running, the page falls back to keyword retrieval and says so.

    How this answers

    • It only answers from the library. Your question is matched against the explainers on this site, all of which come from the whitelist — CPF, MAS, MoneySense, IRAS, MOF, gov.sg.
    • Every answer names its source and links to it.
    • It refuses rather than guesses. A question outside the library gets "I don't know", not an invented answer. A confidently wrong figure spoken in Hokkien would do more damage than this product does good.
    • It explains, it never advises. No answer tells you what to buy. Each one ends with questions you can put to the person selling to you.

    Retrieval is keyword scoring, and it decides what the model is allowed to see. kimi-k3 then answers from those passages only, under a system prompt that forbids naming a figure absent from them, forbids recommending anything, and requires the literal token NO_ANSWER when the passages do not cover the question. Retrieval finding nothing skips the model entirely — there is no permitted context, so there is nothing to answer from.
