Prototype — Hokkien text is a draft, and the voice is a stand-in. Hokkien now has its own colloquial script rather than shared written Chinese, and real Singapore Hokkien narration exists in this repo (MERaLiON, run locally). Until those clips are rendered into site/audio/, Play falls back to reading the Mandarin version in your browser's voice. Teochew has no real voice at all yet. No script here has been through the human accuracy check the problem statement requires, and no native Hokkien speaker has reviewed the dialect text.
