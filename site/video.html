Prototype — draft text, stand-in voice. Hokkien captions are colloquial Hokkien and can be narrated by MERaLiON, A*STAR's Singapore Hokkien model, once the clips are rendered into site/audio/. Until then — and for Teochew always — playback borrows your browser's Mandarin or Cantonese voice so the shape of the experience can be judged. Scripts are drafts, unchecked by a native speaker.
